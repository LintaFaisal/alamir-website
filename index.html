FAQ

Common questions

What documents do I need to register?

Emirates ID or passport with visa, a passport photo, and an eye test (we can do this at our branch).

Can I convert my foreign licence?

Yes. We'll check whether your licence converts directly or needs a short refresher course.

How long does it take to get a licence?

Between 40 and 80 days depending on your package: Premium 40 days, Standard 60 days, Basic 80 days.

What is the difference between a VIP and a normal test?

VIP tests come with the Standard and Premium packages and get you earlier test dates. Ask us on WhatsApp for details.
